# IGCSE course coverage

Inspected 24 October 2026: [FocusPhysics 0625 checklist](https://focusphysics.com/syllabus/0625/), Year 10 and 11 hubs, all ten topic hubs and their 78 linked lesson pages. The checklist links 324 statements to 62 distinct lessons; topic hubs add three practical/extension lessons and thirteen revision/review pages. Year 9 and A Level pages are outside this IGCSE audit.

The lab now has 41 working models (28 new). Every lesson below has a model entry point, original teaching notes, worked calculations and questions. **A lesson mapping means an exploration supporting that lesson, not that every one of its syllabus statements is independently simulated.** Safety, resource choices, stellar evolution and particle arrangements include explicitly qualitative material. Simulations do not replace hands-on practical work or the teacher’s notes. Original slides and resources remain linked on FocusPhysics; they are not republished.

## Scientific limitations

Each model documents its physical method and visual scaling in Equations & model. Exact analytic solutions are used where available. New stochastic decay uses seeded exponential waiting times. Gas particles, diffraction wavefronts, convection circulation, scattering paths and stellar stages are illustrative; their accompanying numeric quantities use the stated equations. See README.md and tests/curriculum.test.mjs for validation.
