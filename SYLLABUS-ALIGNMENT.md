# Cambridge learning-path alignment

Checked 27 October 2026 against the official PDFs. These are selected section links, not full outcome-level coverage or Cambridge endorsement. Teaching text, tasks and questions are original.

- [Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625, 2026–2028, version 2](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697209-2026-2028-syllabus.pdf)
- [Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics 9702, 2025–2027, version 1](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664565-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf)
- [Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics 9702, 2028–2030, version 1](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/744626-2028-2030-syllabus.pdf)

The library keeps 57 physics engines and provides 91 learning profiles: 40 IGCSE Extended profiles (Core + Supplement), 48 AS/A Level profiles, and 3 enrichment profiles. Two profiles span both AS and A2 ideas; the browser identifies that progression. No separate solver or duplicated animation is created for a second learning level.

Switching mode preserves the current physical state. It changes objectives, the suggested investigation, a worked conceptual/calculation question and the highlighted live calculations. Recommended setups are separate explicit actions; they reset and pause the experiment. Shared and embedded links preserve level, edition and validated SI parameters. All full-model calculations and original resources remain available, with additional mathematics labelled.

## Classification choices

- SHM equations, RC discharge, quantitative electric fields, photoelectric emission and quantitative charged-particle orbits belong to A2 profiles.
- Pendulum timing supports IGCSE measurement; its nonlinear dynamics and small-angle analysis are progression material.
- IGCSE buoyancy focuses on density, pressure and force balance. Quantitative Archimedes work is mapped to AS 4.3; the fluid-drag solver remains extra modelling.
- The Young modulus and two-dimensional projectile calculations use AS profiles.
- Thin-lens constructions and seasonal geometry have IGCSE profiles, without inventing a corresponding required 9702 unit.
- The binary, three-body and double-pendulum studios are enrichment.
- Bragg crystal-plane algebra, empirical sensor curves, full nonlinear oscillations, spectral Planck calculations and similar additions are identified as extensions even when their parent model supports examined ideas.

The 9702 section identifiers were checked in the 2025–2027 and 2028–2030 editions; this does not claim their entire assessment wording is identical. The edition selector changes official PDF links and page references. IGCSE references use 0625 2026–2028 version 2.

## Limits and practical skills

The online section map shows references without a dedicated model, including medical-imaging topics. Linked sections may still contain outcomes not demonstrated by the model. Teacher explanations, written practice and supervised laboratory work are still needed. Calculated readings and seeded stochastic outcomes are model predictions; they do not constitute experimental measurements or measured uncertainty.

## Validation

Run `npm test` and `npm run check`. Profile tests verify all model classifications, reference IDs in both 9702 editions, valid starting conditions, live calculation IDs, mode/edition URL round-trips and the physical regime of recommended starters. Browser checks verify switching without a state reset, the level-specific lesson resources, expanded/embedded controls and responsive path navigation.
